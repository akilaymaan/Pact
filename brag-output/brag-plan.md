# Brag Plan: Pact

## What is this app?

Pact is an AI-agent marketplace for creator–brand deals: every creator and every brand has an agent that scouts opportunities, matches counterparties, negotiates terms, and prepares proposals — but the deal only moves when a human approves it. Payment is protected, outcomes are verified.

## The angle

The inversion: **agents do the work, humans keep the pen.** Every AI product demo screams autonomy; Pact's whole identity is the one line "AI agents propose. Humans approve." The video dramatizes a deal assembling itself — scouting, matching, negotiating — and then stopping, waiting, until the human stamps it. The tension between machine speed and human control is the story.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

The site's own hook, full-bleed on cobalt: "LET AGENTS LAND DEALS." slams in line by line in the pixel display face, then "YOU DECIDE." lands in the periwinkle accent with a hard stop. Big, confident, immediate — it is the entire product thesis in six words.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **The workspace card fills itself.** The tilted paper "deal document" (Exhibit 001) lands on cobalt: pipeline nodes C → PACT → B connect, log events check off one by one ("Opportunity discovered", "Audience fit confirmed", "Deal terms prepared"), and the proposal block materializes — 1 Instagram Reel, $4,500, 7 days, +$1,000 bonus.
- **Terms negotiate themselves.** Two agent tags (C / B) exchange proposals — counter-offer chips tick through values and settle on "$4,500 + $1,000 performance bonus". Nobody typed anything.
- **The stamp.** Status chip reads "Your decision" → a cursor clicks "Approve deal" → "Approved by you" stamp slams onto the proposal, paired with the statement "AI proposes. You approve."

## Outro / punchline

The workflow strip accelerates through SCOUT → MATCH → NEGOTIATE → APPROVE → PROTECT → DELIVER → VERIFY → GET PAID, resolving to the PACT mark and "Your agent. Your next deal." with the quiet footnote: payment protected, outcomes verified.

## User flow worth showing

Proposal prepared by agents → human approves → payment protected / outcome verified. The video's centerpiece (scenes 2–4) recreates exactly this flow from the product's own demos — the workspace proposal card, the negotiation exchange, and the approval stamp — rather than describing them.

## Tone

- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "quiet premium product film — cobalt field, paper artifacts, pixel-type confidence"
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds, deliberate entrances. Confidence through restraint: hard cuts only at the stamp and the workflow acceleration. No whoosh-spam, no feature-list montage.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080

## Duration: ~20s

## Share copy (draft)

## Audio direction

- Role: warm, confident corporate bed under everything; restrained motion-matched SFX on key landings
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (bundled; steady and clean, ~110 BPM — the `polished`/`cinematic` pick)
- Music treatment: fade in fast at t=0, sit low under the visuals, slight lift into the outro, clean fade on the logo hold
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read — track tempo ~110 BPM, beat grid from 0.56s at ~0.55s spacing; strong cues at 8.74, 10.93, 13.11, 17.47, 18.56, 22.93s in track time. Natural locks: scene-1 headline slams (~0.56/1.09/1.64), the scene-4 stamp (~scene start + strong cue), and the outro resolve (~17.47/18.56). Beat-grid (~0.55s spacing) can drive the scene-2 checklist ticks and the scene-5 workflow word-strip.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none to subtle — at most a faint glow breathe on the cobalt field behind the hero card; no waveform theatrics
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched. A soft card-place/deal sound when the workspace card lands; dry ticks for checklist/log events; a paper-hit or stamp thunk on "Approved by you"; one clean rise into the outro. The casino/card SFX family fits the "deal" motif.
- Audio-coupled moments: headline line slams (scene 1), checklist ticks (scene 2), counter-value ticks (scene 3), the stamp hit (scene 4), workflow word-strip acceleration (scene 5)
- Restraint rule: audio never outruns the visuals — no risers, no EDM drops, nothing that makes a calm product feel anxious. Silence is allowed in the half-beat before the stamp.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The thesis — 3.5s

Cobalt field, faint dotted grid. Pixel-type lines clip-reveal upward, staggered hard: "LET AGENTS" / "LAND DEALS." then "YOU DECIDE." in periwinkle — the exact hero treatment from the site, cropped tight so type fills the frame.

Sequential/interaction: yes — three lines land one by one, ~0.4s apart, each with a low confident hit.
Audio intent: establishes authority; music enters with the first line.
Audio-coupled idea: each line slam on/near a beat; "YOU DECIDE." gets a strong cue.
Music: bed fades in under.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2 (cut on beat).

### Scene 2 — The deal assembles itself — 5s

The paper workspace card ("Exhibit 001" ticket, tilted with offset cobalt shadow) lands center-frame. Inside it, the deal builds: pipeline nodes C → PACT → B connect with a packet pulse; three log events tick to "Done" one by one ("Opportunity discovered", "Audience fit confirmed", "Deal terms prepared"); the proposal block rises — "1 Instagram Reel", "$4,500 · 7 days · +$1,000 bonus" — and its status badge flips "Preparing" → "Your review".

Sequential/interaction: yes — log events check off sequentially (~0.5s apart), then the proposal materializes. This is the recreated product surface, the centerpiece.
Audio intent: quiet momentum — ticks for each checklist item, a soft card-place when the document lands.
Audio-coupled idea: checklist ticks on the beat grid; proposal block lands on a strong cue.
Transition mood: clean slide/wipe → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The negotiation — 3s

Cropped negotiation surface: "CREATOR AGENT" and "BRAND AGENT" headers over a compact exchange — proposal chips alternate sides ("$3,800 flat" → "$4,500 + $1,000 performance bonus" → "Agreed"), each arriving like a dealt card. Ends on "Terms agreed" chip.

Sequential/interaction: yes — three alternating chips, fast but each settled ~0.6s so they're readable.
Audio intent: the "back-and-forth" made audible — alternating soft card sounds per chip.
Audio-coupled idea: chip arrivals on alternating beats.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The human in the loop — 3.5s

The proposal card again, tight crop on the decision row: chip reads "Your decision", three buttons (Decline / Request changes / Approve deal). A cursor glides in, clicks "Approve deal"; a half-beat of held silence, then the "✓ Approved by you" stamp SLAMS onto the card with a paper-hit, and "AI proposes." / "You approve." resolves beside it.

Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated cursor click + stamp.
Audio intent: the video's climax — drop the bed for a breath before the stamp, hit on impact.
Audio-coupled idea: stamp lands exactly on a strong cue.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — The machine, named — 4.5s

The workflow strip runs once through, accelerating: SCOUT → MATCH → NEGOTIATE → **APPROVE** (held a beat in accent) → PROTECT → DELIVER → VERIFY → GET PAID — then resolves to the PACT pixel mark, "Your agent. Your next deal." and the quiet line "Payment protected. Outcomes verified." plus a small mono footer "pact · the agent-to-agent marketplace".

Sequential/interaction: yes — the stage words run as an accelerating strip, APPROVE held in accent.
Audio intent: lift and resolve; music swells slightly then fades under the logo hold.
Audio-coupled idea: stage words snap to the beat grid as they accelerate.
Transition mood: resolve → end.

**Music mood for this video:** confident, upbeat, premium — business-pulse bed, no parody.
**Audio summary:** A warm corporate bed that enters with the headline, ticks along the assembling deal, drops out for a breath before the approval stamp, and lifts into the logo resolve — SFX stays sparse and physical (card deals, ticks, one stamp thunk).
